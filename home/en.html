The school library made easy

BiblioViva is software for running a school library: loans for students and teachers, cataloguing from the ISBN, printing of library cards for patrons, barcodes and spine labels for books. It comes from hands-on experience running a school library, with the aim of a tool that is simple, also for people who seldom use a computer.
Free and open source.

Students' data
stay at school.

BiblioViva runs on a computer at the school and only the staff can reach it. No cloud service, no subscription, no data handed over.

[[icon:key-round]]Staff-only accessNo access for students or families; connections go through a private network.
[[icon:user-round]]AnonymisationWhen a patron leaves the school, their personal data are erased at once.
[[icon:hard-drive-download]]Backup and restoreA daily backup of books, patrons, photos and settings.

The rules of a state school.

An Italian state school is a public administration: data protection, accessibility and the choice of software follow precise rules. For each one, the documentation says what BiblioViva does and what is left to the school.

[[icon:lock-keyhole]]Data protectionGDPR: the school stays the controller and no supplier processes the patrons' data. Ready texts for the record of processing and the notice.
[[icon:accessibility]]AccessibilityTarget WCAG 2.2 AA, beyond what Legge 4/2004 and the AgID guidelines ask today.
[[icon:landmark]]Open source in the PAGPL licence and a publiccode.yml file for the Developers Italia catalogue (CAD, art. 68–69).
[[icon:shield-check]]SecurityAccounts by role, the audit log and the nightly backup, described against AgID's minimum measures.

What BiblioViva does.

Everything a school library needs, area by area.

[[icon:arrow-right-left]]Loans

  • Loans and returns with the barcode scanner
  • Renewals, limits and due dates on the school calendar
  • Overdue notices printed by class
  • A wishlist of books

[[icon:book-open]]Catalogue

  • Cataloguing from the ISBN with OPAC SBN and covers
  • Genres, reading levels and automatic call numbers
  • A search that forgives typing mistakes
  • Shelf stocktaking

[[icon:users]]Patrons

  • Students and teachers, organised by class
  • Import from the school office's lists
  • ID photos from a scan
  • Class upgrade at the end of the year

[[icon:printer]]Printing

  • Two-sided library cards, with calibration
  • Spine bands and barcode labels
  • Templates edited with a visual editor
  • Print lists to work in batches

[[icon:chart-bar]]Reports

  • Ready-made reports for the end-of-year review
  • A guided editor and pivot tables
  • Export to a spreadsheet
  • Direct printing

[[icon:settings]]Administration

  • Accounts by role, with separate permissions
  • Every setting from the interface
  • Nightly backup and restore
  • Italian and English, other languages with translation packs
