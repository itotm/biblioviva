# Help

This section is for everyone who uses BiblioViva: it collects the solutions to the most common problems, the meaning of
the technical words and the directions for asking for help when the solutions are not enough.

- [Common problems](problems.md): the address that does not open, the camera that does not start, the scanner that
  types strange characters, the print that is the wrong size, a forgotten password, each with its cause and the steps
  to take.
- [Glossary](glossary.md): the words of BiblioViva and of the installation (copy, call number, card, Tailscale,
  Docker, volume and the others) explained in plain terms.
- [Ask for help](ask-for-help.md): how to write a report on the project's site and what to put in it, without any
  patron data.

For daily use and for the settings, the other sections of the documentation are the
[Manual](../manual/README.md) and the [Administration](../admin/README.md); the installation is in the
[Installation](../setup/README.md) section.
