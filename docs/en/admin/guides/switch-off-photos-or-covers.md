# Switch off photos or covers

This guide explains how to switch off the patrons' photos, the books' covers or both, when the school prefers not to
keep them. It is for whoever administers BiblioViva. The two functions are independent: one can be switched off while
the other is kept.

Switching off deletes nothing: the photos and covers already saved stay and reappear if the function is switched on
again.

- Time: about 3 minutes.
- Account: `admin`.
- Requirements: nothing.

## Procedure

### The patrons' photos

1. Log in to BiblioViva as `admin`.
1. Open **Administration**.
1. Open **Configuration**.
1. In the **Settings** list open **Patron photos**.
1. Untick the **Patron photos** box.
1. Press **Save**, at the top right. The message "Settings saved" appears.

<!-- figure: admin/config-photos | The Patron photos section with the Patron photos box -->

With photos off there is no photo on any page: they are neither taken nor uploaded, the photo census is not there and
the cards come out without a photo.

If photos stay on but are not always needed, the **Photo required** box controls the reminders: when off, the
dashboard, the patron list and the print lists no longer flag who has no photo. Photos can still be taken, uploaded and
printed on the cards.

### The books' covers

1. Go back to **Configuration**.
1. In the **Settings** list open **Covers**.
1. Untick the **Book covers** box.
1. Press **Save**.

With covers off there is no cover on any page: they are not looked up online, nor photographed or uploaded, and the
catalogue does not show them.

## Result

- The pages about photos, or covers, no longer show them and no longer ask for them.
- The files already saved stay on the computer and in the backups.
- To switch the function on again, tick the same box again and press **Save**.

## Possible problems

### The photos already saved must be deleted

Switching the function off does not do it. Deletion is done patron by patron [VERIFY: whether there is a way to delete
all the photos at once].

### The printed cards still have the photo

The cards printed before the change still have the photo: reprint the cards without it.

### A field has an invalid value

"Fix the marked values before saving." appears: a field in the section has an invalid value. Correct it.

### The page is left with unsaved changes

BiblioViva asks for confirmation.
