# Set up the library

This guide explains how to give BiblioViva the library's name, logo, colours and language. It is for whoever administers
BiblioViva, usually right after the [first run](../../setup/steps/first-run.md), where the name, colours and
language were already chosen once.

- Time: about 10 minutes.
- Account: `admin`, the only one that sees **Administration**.
- Requirements: the library's logo, if there is one, in a PNG, JPEG or WebP file. A PNG with a transparent background is
  best.

## Procedure

### Open the page

1. Log in to BiblioViva as `admin`.
1. Open **Administration**.
1. Open **Configuration**.
1. Open **Appearance and language**. The page shows, one below the other, the name, the logo, the language, the area
   style, the colours and the dashboard.

<!-- figure: admin/appearance | The Appearance and language page with the name, the logo, the language and the colour sets -->

### Change the name

1. Type the library's name in the **Name** field. It appears at the top left, on the dashboard, on the printouts and in
   the application installed on the phone. The field is required.
1. Press **Save the name**. The message "Library name saved" appears.

### Add the logo

1. In the **Library logo** panel press **Upload the logo**.
1. Choose the image file. The logo appears at once at the top left of every page, next to the library's name.
1. To replace it, press **Change the logo** and choose another file.
1. To remove it, press **Remove the logo**. A generic icon takes its place.

The logo is stored in the configuration: it is part of the [configuration export](export-the-configuration.md). The
name and logo of BiblioViva appear only on the dashboard.

### Choose the colours

1. In the **Colour set** panel choose a set. The preview shows at once on the page and the "Current set" label above the
   sets marks the one in use.
1. Press **Save the colours**. The message "Colours saved" appears.

The colours change only the look, not the layout of the pages, and stay legible in black and white. The style of the
band at the top is chosen in the **Area style** panel: the choice applies at once.

### Change the language

BiblioViva uses one language for the whole installation: the choice applies to all the staff.

1. In the **Application language** panel choose the language in the **Language** list.
1. Press **Change language**. The page reloads in the new language.

For a language that is not in the list see [Add a language](add-a-language.md).

## Result

- The name and logo appear at the top left of every page.
- The chosen colours apply to all the accounts.
- The chosen language applies to all the staff.

## Possible problems

- The **Save the name** button stays off: the name is empty or the same as the one already saved. The message "Type the
  library's name." appears when the field is empty.
- The logo is rejected with "Format not accepted: use PNG, JPEG or WebP.": convert the image to one of these formats.
- The logo is rejected with "The file is too large": reduce the size of the image with a photo-editing program and try
  again.
- Other library settings, such as the contact email, the time zone and the start of the school year, are in the
  **Library and school** section of the [configuration](../screens/configuration.md).
