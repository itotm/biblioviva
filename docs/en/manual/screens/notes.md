# Notes

**Notes** are coloured slips with which people working in the library leave a notice for colleagues and for the
administrator: "The desk scanner does not read the cards", "The label paper has run out". They are read and written by
all accounts; only the administrator archives them and pins them to the dashboard. The page is opened with **Notes** in
the side menu (on the phone under **More**) or with the **Open the notes** button of the pinned notes. For the
step-by-step procedure see [Leave a note for colleagues](../guides/leave-a-note.md).

The page has two tabs: **Open** and **Archived**.

## Open notes

The **Open** tab shows the slips that are still valid, the pinned ones first. Above the slips: "`<n>` notes". Without
notes: "No open notes", with the **New note** button. To accounts that are not administrators the page reminds: "Only
the administrator archives notes and pins them to the dashboard."

## Slip

Each slip shows:

- **Pinned**, with the pin, if the administrator has pinned it to the dashboard;
- the text, with line breaks; a long text is shortened and opens with **Show all** (and closes again with
  **Show less**);
- the account that wrote it and when ("`<account>` · `<date and time>`");
- in archived notes, also who archived it and when ("Archived by `<account>` · `<date and time>`").

A note cannot be edited. In case of a mistake, ask the administrator to archive it and write another one.

The administrator also sees two buttons on the slip:

- **Pin to the dashboard** / **Unpin**: the note appears at the top of the dashboard (and at the top of **Loans** for
  whoever opens the desk directly) until it is unpinned or archived. "Note pinned" or "Note unpinned" appears.
- **Archive**: moves the note to the **Archived** tab. "Note archived" appears.

## New note

The **New note** button opens a window with these fields:

- **Text**: the text of the note (hint: "E.g. The desk scanner does not read the cards."), up to 500 characters; next
  to the field a counter shows "`<n>`/500". If the text is missing: "Write the text of the note."; if it is too long:
  "The text is longer than 500 characters."
- **Colour**: **Yellow** (default), **Pink**, **Green**, **Light blue** or **Orange**. The colour is only decoration
  and changes nothing.
- **Pinned to the dashboard**: for the administrator only. "Shown to everyone at the top of the dashboard (and of the
  circulation desk) until it is unpinned or archived."

Under the text the window warns: "Every account reads the notes: do not write pupils' personal data." and "A note
cannot be edited: in case of a mistake, ask the administrator to archive it and write another one." The **Add** button
saves and "Note added" appears; **Cancel** closes the window.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Do not write students' names or personal data in notes: all accounts read them.

## Archived notes

The **Archived** tab shows the archived notes, the last archived first, 48 at a time: above the slips "`<n>` of
`<total>` archived notes" and, if there are more, the **Show more** button. Without archived notes: "No archived
notes". All accounts read them; the administrator sees two buttons on each slip:

- **Restore**: puts the note back among the open ones ("Note restored").
- **Delete**: deletes the note, after the confirmation "Delete the note?" with the text "It is erased for good and
  cannot be recovered." On confirmation "Note deleted" appears.

## Pinned notes

Pinned notes also appear outside this page, with the title **Notes** and the **Open the notes** button: at the top of
the [dashboard](dashboard.md) and, for accounts that open the desk directly (such as "Loan management"), at the top of
[Loans](checkout.md). On the administrator's dashboard the **Open notes** tile counts those still to be handled.
