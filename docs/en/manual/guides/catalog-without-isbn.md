# Catalogue a book without an ISBN

This guide explains how to add to the catalogue a book that has no ISBN, for example an old book, a comic, a book made
by the school or a DVD: the record is searched by title and author or written by hand. It is for whoever catalogues the
books.

- Time: 2 to 5 minutes per book.
- Account: "Cataloguing" (also "Administration").
- Requirements: the book, with the title page in front to copy title, author and publisher; the Internet connection of
  the BiblioViva computer for the search by title; the USB barcode scanner for a DVD that has an EAN code.

A book without an ISBN has no barcode of its own that BiblioViva can read at the desk. Each copy receives a code from
BiblioViva, which is printed on a label to stick on the book: see [Print book labels](print-book-labels.md).

## Procedure

### Search the record by title and author

1. Open **Catalogue** and press **New book**.
1. In **Material** choose the type of material, if it is not the one already selected.
1. Press **No code**. The page "Search by title and author" opens.

   <!-- figure: catalog/new-book-search | The search by title and author of the New book page -->

1. Write the **Title**, the **Author** or both, then press **Search**. One of the two fields is enough: if both are
   empty, "Type at least the title or the author." appears.
1. Wait for the **Records found** list. The records are in order of completeness: the first one carries the label
   **Most complete**. Each record shows title, author, publisher, year and description.
1. Compare the record with the book in hand (year, publisher, number of pages) and press **Use this record** on the
   right one. The **Record and copies** form opens with the data already written.
1. Go on from the check of the data as in [Catalogue a new book](catalog-a-new-book.md): series, classification,
   copies, **Save record and copies**.

### Fill in the record by hand

When no record matches the book, or the list is empty, the record is written by hand.

1. Press **Fill in by hand** on the page "Search by title and author". From the list of the records found the same
   button is called **Enter by hand**. The form opens, with the notice "Record filled in by hand.".

   <!-- figure: catalog/new-book-manual | The form of the record filled in by hand -->

1. Write the **Title**, as on the title page. Write the **Subtitle** only if there is one.
1. If the book is a translation, write in **Original title** the title in the original language.
1. Write the **Author** as "Surname, Name". Without a personal author press "Anonimo" (unknown author) or "AA. VV."
   (work by several authors).
1. Write the **Publisher**. If it is unknown press "s.n." (no name). Write the **Place** if it is known, otherwise
   press "s.l." (no place).
1. Write the **Year** of publication with four digits. With an unknown year leave the field empty.
1. Write in the **Physical description** pages, illustrations and size, on a single line (for example "120 p. : ill. ;
   24 cm").
1. If the book belongs to a series, write **Series** and **Number in the series**.
1. Choose the classification and write the copies as in [Catalogue a new book](catalog-a-new-book.md), then press
   **Save record and copies**.

### DVDs and other material with an EAN code

1. Open **New book** and choose in **Material** the right type, for example DVD.
1. Read with the scanner the barcode of the case. BiblioViva recognises that it is an EAN and not an ISBN: it skips the
   lookup, opens the form and writes the code in the **EAN (not an ISBN)** field, with the notice "EAN … read: it is not
   an ISBN, so the record must be filled in by hand.".
1. Fill in the record as described above and press **Save record and copies**.

## Result

The book has a record and one or more copies with the barcode generated by BiblioViva. The next step is to print the
labels and stick them on: until the book has the code on its cover, it cannot be read at the circulation desk.

## Possible problems

### The search by title finds nothing

"No record found. The record can be filled in by hand." appears. Try with fewer words or with the author's surname
only (press **New code** and repeat **No code**), or fill in the record by hand.

### The service cannot be reached

"The service cannot be reached…" appears: the BiblioViva computer cannot reach OPAC SBN. Press **Retry** or fill in the
record by hand: data written by hand are as valid as those found.

### The year is implausible

Saving says "Implausible year": the year is not a number between 1450 and next year. Correct it or leave the field
empty.

### The ISBN is not valid

Saving says "Invalid ISBN (check the digits)": the book has an ISBN but it was written wrongly. Read it again with the
scanner or correct the digits in the **ISBN** field.

### Author and publisher are missing

They are mandatory. Use "Anonimo", "AA. VV.", "s.n." or "s.l.", which BiblioViva recognises as allowed values.
