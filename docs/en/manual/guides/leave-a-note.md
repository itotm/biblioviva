# Leave a note for colleagues

This page explains how to write a note, a coloured slip that every account reads, to alert colleagues or the
administrator to a problem or a useful piece of information: the barcode scanner that does not read, a book to check, a change
of hours. It is for anyone who works with BiblioViva, with any account.

- Time: about a minute.
- Account: any.
- Requirements: the text of the notice, at most 500 characters.

## Procedure

1. Open **Notes** from the side menu (on the phone from **More**). The page opens with the open notes as coloured
   slips; the most important ones, pinned, are at the top.

   <!-- figure: notes/open | The Notes page with some coloured slips -->

1. Press **New note**. A window opens.
1. Write the notice in the **Text** field. Under the field a counter says how many characters have been used.
1. Choose a **Colour**: Yellow, Pink, Green, Light blue or Orange. The colour only serves to recognise the slip at a
   glance.

   <!-- figure: notes/new-dialog | The New note window -->

1. Press **Add**. A message says **Note added** and the slip appears among the open notes, with the name of the account
   that wrote it and the date.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Notes are visible to all accounts: do not write personal data of pupils.

> [!NOTE]
> The slip carries the name of the account (for example "Loan management"), not that of the person: to be contacted
> again, sign the notice in the text.

## Result

The note stays among the open notes until the administrator archives it. If the administrator pins it, it appears to
everyone at the top of the dashboard; for the "Loan management" account, which opens directly on the circulation desk,
it appears at the top of the **Loans** page.

Everyone reads the notes; only the "Administration" account pins them with **Pin to the dashboard** (and unpins them
with **Unpin**), archives them with **Archive**, restores them with **Restore** or deletes them with **Delete**.
Archived notes are in the **Archived** tab.

## Possible problems

### The text is too long

The window shows the counter in red and says that the text exceeds 500 characters. Shorten the notice or write two.

### There is a mistake in the text

A note cannot be edited. Ask the administrator to archive it and write another.

### The slip is long and cannot be read in full

Long slips are shortened: press **Show all** to read them in full, **Show less** to fold them again.

### The note is no longer valid

Ask the administrator to archive it. An archived slip can still be consulted in the **Archived** tab until the
administrator deletes it for good.

### An urgent problem

A note does not alert anyone: colleagues read it the next time they open the page or the dashboard (if it is pinned).
For an urgent problem also talk to the administrator in person.
