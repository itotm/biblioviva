# Use the print lists

This page explains how to gather patrons in a print list, to print their cards in one go and keep track of who has
already had them. It is for whoever prepares the cards: for example the cards of new enrolments, of whoever has not
received one yet, of a reading group. There are also lists of copies, for the books' labels: see [Print book
labels](print-book-labels.md).

- Time: about 5 minutes.
- Account: Cards and photos (also Administration). The patron lists are seen with the permission to manage patrons; the
  book lists with the one to manage the catalogue.
- Requirements: no material. To add people with the scanner their cards are needed, already printed.

## Procedure

### Create a list

1. Log in to BiblioViva with the "Cards and photos" account.
1. Open **Printing**. The page lists the lists, each with the kind, the number of entries and the date of the last
   printing or "Never printed".
1. Press **New patron list**. The "New list of patrons" window opens.

   <!-- figure: printing/lists-index | The Printing page with the patron lists and the New list of patrons window -->

1. Write in the **List name** field a name that recalls the purpose, for example "New enrolments". If left empty,
   BiblioViva proposes a name such as "List 3 of …" with today's date.
1. Press **New patron list** in the window. The page of the list opens, still empty.

### Fill the list

The three ways described below can be used together. Whoever is already in the list is not added twice.

### Fill the list with the filters

<!-- figure: printing/list-filters | The page of a patron list with the Add with filters panel -->

1. In the **Add with filters** panel choose what is needed. **Year** and the section field limit to one class; **Type**
   to students or teachers. In a school with several stages there is also **Stage**.
1. To get only whoever has not received the card, switch on **Only those whose card was never printed**.
1. To get only whoever has no photo, switch on **Only those without a photo**. The choice appears only if the
   administrator has switched on the photo reminder.
1. To get the new enrolments of an import, choose the import in **Import**. For whoever was entered from a certain date,
   give the date in **Created from**.
1. Press **Add to the list**. The notice "N added, M already present" appears. With "No patron matches the filters."
   nobody was added.

### Fill the list with the barcode scanner

1. In the **Add with the scanner** panel click in the reading field.
1. Read each patron's card with the scanner. Under the field "… added" or "… is already in the list" appears.

### Fill the list from the Patrons page

1. Open **Patrons** and select the rows by holding down Ctrl, or Shift for a range, while pressing on the row.
1. Press **Add selected to a list**. In the "Add to a print list" window choose an **Existing list** or a **New list** with
   its **Name of the new list**, and press **Add**.

From the **Patron record** the same operation is the **Add to a list** button.

### Arrange the list

1. Check the list: each entry shows the patron and whether the card is printed ("yes" or "no").
1. To change the print order, drag the entries or use **Move up** and **Move down**.
1. To remove an entry press **Remove from the list**. The patron stays in the archive.
1. To change the name press **Rename**, write the new name and press **Save**.

### Print the cards

1. In the page of the list press **Print cards**. The **Print cards** window opens, which shows the number of cards.
1. Choose how to print and press **Print**, then follow the instructions of [Print the cards of a
   class](print-class-cards.md#choose-how-to-print).

### Mark the cards as printed

Printing alone marks nothing. When the cards have come out of the printer well:

1. To empty the list at once, switch on **Empty the list after printing**. Otherwise leave the box off.
1. Press **Mark as printed**. The notice "N cards marked as printed" appears.

The date of the last printing is read under the name of the list. The lists with cards still to print are counted in the
**Print lists** icon of the top bar; its menu opens a list or **All lists**.

### Close the list

1. To reuse it later leave it as it is.
1. To remove all its entries press **Empty** and confirm with **Empty** in the "Empty the list?" window. The patrons stay
   in the archive.
1. To delete the list press **Delete** and confirm with **Delete** in the "Delete the list …?" window.

## Result

The list contains the chosen patrons, in the wanted order. The printed cards show "yes" in the **Card printed** column,
and the **Printing** list shows the date of the last printing. The **Patrons without a printed card** number of the
Dashboard goes down.

## Possible problems

### The button to add to the list is off

The **Add to the list** button of the filters panel is off: no filter has been chosen.

### No patron with the card read

With the scanner, "No patron with card …" appears: the number read belongs to nobody. Read the card again.

### The code read is not a card

"The scanned code is not a card" appears: another kind of code was read, for example that of a book.

### The button to print cards is off

"Print cards" is off: the list is empty. Add the patrons first.

### Lists are missing in the Printing page

The lists are missing in the **Printing** page: the account sees only the lists of its own kind. The book lists require
the permission to manage the catalogue.

### The cards do not come out as expected

See the possible problems of [Print the cards of a class](print-class-cards.md#possible-problems).
