# Mark closures in the calendar

This page explains how to consult the library calendar, how to add dates to remember (a reading in a class, a contest
deadline) and how to mark the days when the library is closed, which move loan due dates. Dates to remember are written
by whoever works at the desk; closures are marked by the administrator.

- Time: a few minutes.
- Account: to consult the calendar, any account. To add dates to remember, "Loan management" or "Administration". To
  mark closures, "Administration" only.
- Requirements: the dates of the school's closures (holidays, bridge days, days without lessons).

## Procedure

### Consult the calendar

1. Open **Calendar** from the tabs at the top of the circulation desk. The other accounts open it from the dashboard,
   in the **Upcoming dates** tile, with **Open the calendar**.
1. Read the school year shown in the title. To change year press the button with the previous or the next year, at the
   sides of the title.

   <!-- figure: calendar/year | The calendar of a school year with closures and dates to remember -->

1. Look at the months. Under each month appear the number of open days, the closures with their reason and the dates
   to remember. The colours and the text are read in the **Legend**: **Weekly closing day**, **Holiday**,
   **Closure period** and **Date to remember**.

### Add a date to remember

1. Press **New date**. The **New date** window opens.
1. Write the **Title**, for example a reading in a class.
1. Choose the **Day**. For a period of several days also choose **Until**.
1. If needed, write a **Note**.
1. Press **Save**. A message says **Date added** and the date appears under its month, with the calendar icon.

   <!-- figure: calendar/new-date | The New date window -->

1. To correct a date, click its title, change the fields and press **Save**. To delete it press **Delete** and
   confirm.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The dates are visible to all accounts: do not write personal data of pupils. Dates to remember do not change loan
> due dates.

### Mark closing days (administrator)

1. With the "Administration" account, open **Administration**, then **Configuration**, then **Calendar**.
1. To close a day, click that day in the calendar of the **Library calendar** block. To reopen a day closed by hand,
   click again.
1. To close several days in a row, press **Add a period**, choose **From** and **To** and write the **Reason**.
1. Press **Save**. A message says **Calendar saved**.

The closing days of every week (**Days closed every week**) and the national holidays are already in the calendar.
The details are in [Set the school calendar](../../admin/guides/set-the-calendar.md).

## Result

Closures appear in the **Calendar** with their reason. A loan whose due date falls on a closed day has its due date
moved to the first open day and the desk says so in a message. Dates to remember also appear in the **Upcoming dates**
tile of the dashboard, when at most thirty days are left.

## Possible problems

### The New date button is not there

Only the "Loan management" and "Administration" accounts can add dates. The other accounts read the calendar without
being able to change it.

### Closures cannot be changed from the calendar

From the circulation desk the calendar does not change closures: the "Administration" account and the
**Administration** → **Configuration** → **Calendar** page are needed.

### Loans already recorded do not change due date

A loan's due date is calculated with the calendar of the day the book is lent: a closure added afterwards does not move
the due dates of loans already in progress. To move a due date that falls on a closing day, renew the loan.

### The date to remember does not appear in Upcoming dates

The dashboard shows the dates of the next thirty days: a more distant date appears later.

### The title is too long or the period is wrong

The window marks the field: shorten the title, or choose an **Until** that is not earlier than the **Day**.
